Nelson Wasswa is a Ugandan technologist, researcher, and nonprofit leader working at the intersection of emerging technology, data, public institutions, and sustainable development in Africa. He is the Founder and Executive Director of [Sodzo Foundation] (https://sodzofoundation.org), where he leads initiatives that use technology, geospatial intelligence, open-source tools, and data-driven approaches to strengthen evidence-based decision-making, institutional capacity, environmental governance, climate resilience, and public health.
Nelson’s work is grounded in a central question: how can African institutions prepare for and responsibly govern rapidly advancing technologies while ensuring that their benefits translate into better public outcomes? His experience spans technology-enabled research, digital public goods, environmental intelligence, community-generated data, and partnerships involving researchers, communities, and public-interest actors. This has given him practical experience in translating emerging technologies and evidence into institutional and policy-relevant action.
He is particularly interested in the institutional implications of transformative technologies, including the capacity of governments and public systems to assess risks, build appropriate governance frameworks, develop trusted data infrastructure, strengthen technical capabilities, and ensure that technological change contributes to inclusive development.
Nelson has been recognised through international leadership and fellowship programmes, including selection as a [Shuttleworth Foundation Fellow] (https://www.shuttleworthfoundation.org/fellows/nelson-wasswa) and appointment as a Green Ambassador for Climate in Africa by [Climate Change Africa Opportunities (CCAO)] (https://www.climatechangeafricaopportunities.org/en). He is a previous member of the of the [AidData](https://aiddata.org) Summer Fellowship 2014 and served as a research associate of the [Artificial Intelligence & Data Science Lab](https://air.ug) at Makerere University in Uganda and also former Off-shore SharePoint business analyst and developer at [Application Training Solutions (ACTS)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/application-consulting-training-solutions-acts-recognized-as-winner-for-2019-microsoft-us-partner-award-intelligent-cloud--azure-influencer-300877552.html) in Jacksonville, Florida, USA. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from [Makerere University] (https://www.mak.ac.ug).
Through Sodzo Foundation and his broader research and partnership work, Nelson is committed to building African institutions that are capable of anticipating technological disruption, generating trusted evidence, and governing emerging technologies responsibly and in the public interest.
